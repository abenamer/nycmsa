What are our chances?

Answer a few questions and we replay the middle school admissions lottery 200,000 times with your child's number. You get one chance of an offer for each program on your list.

Everything here uses last year's admissions numbers as reported on the MySchools website: seats, applicants, and offers for each priority group. This year's seats, applicants, and rules can differ.
