NYC Middle School Admissions

What are the chances of your child getting into the NYC middle school of your choice?

Not a Department of Education website. A parent with a child applying to middle school built this for his own family and opened it to others. The estimates are his, not the DOE's.

Answer a few questions and we replay the middle school admissions lottery 200,000 times with your child's number. You get one chance of an offer for each program on your list.

Everything here uses last year's admissions numbers as reported on the MySchools website: seats, applicants, and offers for each priority group. This year's seats, applicants, and rules can differ. The results are estimates from a simplified model, not official DOE figures; each results page lists the caveats.

What are the first two digits of your lottery number?
New York City Public Schools assigns your child a random number as soon as you start an application. Find it in MySchools under "Edit Profile", just above your child's name: a long string of letters and digits such as B51920AF-F1C6-…. The first two characters are enough here. Leave it blank if you have not started an application yet. Random numbers in admissions (NYCPS).
