What each program is about

For every school in the catalog we read its MySchools page: the school overview, the course lists, and each program's description. The "kind of program" tags come from whole words found in the texts, each with a confidence. A match in the admissions method, the program name, the program description, or the school name counts a lot; a match in the school overview counts a little, because that text describes the whole school. A kind is a tag at 50% or more; weaker signals are shown with their percentage, and every match is listed. {{ total }} programs have a profile. Read the texts yourself before you trust a tag.
